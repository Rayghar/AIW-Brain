# Prepare review, preserve human authority

An agent may organize evidence, draft a candidate explanation, identify contradictions and prepare a review pack. It may not approve architecture, waive policy, grant licence clearance or promote knowledge.

A review pack should contain the candidate statement, immutable source passage references, conditions and limitations, contradictory evidence, provenance gaps, licence status, and the real identities and decisions of the responsible reviewers when those decisions exist.

Use AIW's existing governed knowledge lifecycle for authoritative changes. Knowledge Room has no approve or promote button and does not write authority records. A source object's historical approval label is not reissued as approval by this tool.

Independent review, legal clearance, four-eyes approval and production acceptance remain their respective human authorities' responsibilities. Leave each pending until supported by real evidence.
