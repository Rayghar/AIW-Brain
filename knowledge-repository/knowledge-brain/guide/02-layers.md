# Keep evidence and understanding distinct

The evidence layer is the existing AIW acquisition repository. Preserve its snapshots, manifests, passage identifiers and restrictions. Do not rewrite those files to make them easier to browse.

The understanding layer is your Markdown vault: source notes, concepts, questions and review preparation. A source note summarizes one selected source. A concept note compares ideas and records the conditions under which they apply. Neither becomes authoritative by being well written.

Knowledge Room generates a third, disposable object: a navigation projection. Its graph links records to their reported repositories. It is not AIW's canonical Design Graph. Delete or rebuild it without changing architecture decisions.

Keep project-specific notes in separate vaults. A tenant/project label records scope; it does not grant access or prove ownership. Never combine confidential project files into a shared catalogue.
