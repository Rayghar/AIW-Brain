## Product contract

One project graph, the existing eleven chapters and Work / Model / Validate / Output. Companion and Mind Factory retain the selected context. Changes use the existing canonical commands, preview, review, currentness and history. New capability must deepen an existing task rather than add a competing workspace, authority or collection of unrelated controls.

## Customer sequence

1. Follow a requirement or journey and establish its consequential unknown facts once.
2. Read supported interactions between its contracts, authorities, runtime paths and accepted design patterns.
3. Preserve a coherent proposed architecture and its common scenario assumptions; compare its consequences against the current design or another saved alternative.
4. Accept a reviewed change through the established native command path.
5. Express an important architecture obligation as a scoped check and relate observed evidence to the exact design, environment and release.
6. Reconcile changed supporting sources, external models and shared enterprise dependencies without losing identity or historical decisions.

## Interaction rules

- Reuse existing typography, spacing, forms, tables, disclosures, dialogs and model selections.
- Lead with the decision, changed consequence or next action; method and provenance detail remains inspectable.
- Preserve keyboard focus, input, scroll and selected object across previews and asynchronous work.
- A missing prerequisite has a meaningful native action. A disabled action explains what is needed.
- Preserve proposed, accepted, observed, reviewed and approved as distinct states.
- Unsupported behaviour stays unknown. Estimates retain assumptions; synthetic evidence stays labelled.
