The working SDD includes the history; frozen SDD baselines retain their captured content.

## Boundaries and next work

This increment does not complete the roadmap. It extends reviewed edits to saved records in four existing editors. It does not yet persist unaccepted drafts as named alternatives, merge multiple record proposals, generate application/technology designs from evidence, or add general standalone relationship-edit previews. New records and existing pattern proposals retain their established workflows. Source control and approval operations remain separate from design acceptance. No external LLM, document extraction, enterprise collaboration, production capacity claim or audience change is introduced.

Next: deepen coordinated application/technology co-authoring and durable alternative comparison within these same model surfaces, preserving project grounding, explicit human acceptance and the living SDD.
