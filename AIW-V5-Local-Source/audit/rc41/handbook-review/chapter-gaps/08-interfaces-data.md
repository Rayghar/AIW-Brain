# Chapter 8 · Interfaces & data

Part of the AIW handbook gap review (AIW-HANDBOOK-GAP-REVIEW-2026-10-30.md). Gaps verified against the AIW source (paths relative to AIW-V5-Local-Source) and challenged by a skeptic; handbook references are "Handbook › heading (section id)".


**How AIW compares with the handbooks.** For a single contract, Chapter 8 is AIW's closest match to the handbooks. The rule-checked execution agreement combines delivery, publication, repetition, effect-level repeat protection, a timeout kept 'unresolved', owned outcome discovery, one retry owner, the caller envelope and nested attempt exposure, all without an LLM and from inspected sources. The rule-based design tasks (Interaction, Publication, Reads, Persistence, Resilience, Distribution, Partitioning) compare mechanisms and write every option as a Chapter 3 alternative with estimates and pending verification obligations. The doctrine holds throughout: values are stated by a person, open questions stay open, and Sol only proposes. The handbooks go further. They treat each boundary as several connected contracts (authority, meaning, identity, state, time and failure), each recorded as a structured artefact and checked against the others. AIW holds most of this as prose that is checked only for presence, or as design-task answers that leave no structured field for Validate, Model or the SDD to read. Several things are missing outright: a data role and writer inventory, invariant enforcement points, lifecycle guards and owners, a reconciliation design behind 'manual-reconciliation', a semantic field contract, an identity register, a partner guarantee ledger and a version-coexistence record. The cross-record checks that make a design defensible are also absent: retention and recognition horizons against retry, replay and recovery windows; a derived copy's classification against its source; invariant guards against every writer; failover and restore against unresolved outcomes; fleet retry exposure and admission limits against dependency ceilings. Contract timeoutMs and the execution caller budget also remain two unreconciled timing authorities, and the execution agreement itself is optional. Data-side selection is thin and the chapter order works against the method. Store choice weighs only relational records against an aggregate document, no workload profile is recorded, and Chapter 8 guarantees never reach Chapter 7 as fit criteria, even though Chapter 7 configuration facts already name them. Most remedies fit the existing structure as typed fields, rule findings, governed knowledge concerns and SDD tables. Many neighbouring items (sagas, ordering, dead letters, consumer register, operating terms, privacy, residency, master data) are already covered by the deep-dives and are cited as dependencies.

### C8-01 · Derived copies are not designated: no data role, source, freshness bound, permitted use or inherited obligations  
*Severity high · effort L*

- **Handbooks:** Name one authority per fact, then register every other copy (cache, projection, replica, search or vector index, analytical copy, snapshot). For each copy record its source version, freshness measure (expiry, lag, apply position and watermark are not interchangeable), rebuild source, and correction and deletion propagation. Classify each consumer use (display, recommendation, provisional, authoritative) so that a copy never authorises spending, entitlement or an invariant, and remove reverse-authority paths. Copies inherit sensitivity, tenant scope and deletion obligations. / Every derivative needs an identity mapping, a source version, a refresh or freshness bound, a rebuild route, correction and deletion propagation, and an access boundary. This covers replicas, caches, search or vector indexes, projections, exports, backups, quarantine stores and checkpoints. A derivative inherits the source's classification, access policy, minimisation, residency and retention obligations and must never become a second business authority. Joins and enrichment can also create sensitive associations that no single input holds. / Each business fact has one authority, and the service map records authority as well as dependency. A data store is authoritative, derived (rebuildable, carrying a freshness contract) or externally observed (not reconstructable by replay). This classification sets recovery order and the acceptable recovery and migration options. Caches, replicas, queues and projections never become authority. The inventory of writers includes jobs and operational scripts. An application restore cannot reverse provider history, so recovery reasoning follows the authority map.
