# Cross-cutting · method, traceability, Validate, SDD, Model, Sol

Part of the AIW handbook gap review (AIW-HANDBOOK-GAP-REVIEW-2026-10-30.md). Gaps verified against the AIW source (paths relative to AIW-V5-Local-Source) and challenged by a skeptic; handbook references are "Handbook › heading (section id)".


**How AIW compares with the handbooks.** AIW's eleven chapters follow the same order as the method handbooks (requirements, drivers, decisions, responsibilities, components, technology, interfaces, security, runtime, review), over one canonical graph with typed relations that every Model view reads. Its strongest cross-cutting features are content-fingerprint invalidation and honest labelling. Records, control and runtime evidence, assurance records, typed checks and Chapter 11 dispositions go stale when what they judged changes. Simulations and calculations are marked as assumptions or analytical estimates, typed obligation checks give pass/fail/unknown/stale bound to an environment and release, and nothing in AIW or Sol approves. The handbooks treat every design claim as a scoped, falsifiable statement with an evidence level and boundary, a guarantee boundary, scoped parameters, invariants placed inside the boundary that enforces them, owned operating roles, a validation scenario with a negative control, and an expiry. AIW holds these as structured records only in islands: the fixed rule-based design-task topics, Chapter 8 execution agreements, Chapter 9 control parameters and evidence stages, Chapter 10 design/exercise evidence, and Chapter 11 typed checks on accepted obligations. Elsewhere they are free text, so they do not form one claim–evidence model across Chapters 2–10. Validate tests completeness rather than adequacy: findings are only error or warning, warnings never block readiness, missing evidence has no 'unknown' or 'unproven' state, and every record gets the same rigour whatever its consequence. Composition across mechanisms and chapters is thin. Beyond driver-target agreement, binding Chapter 9 controls into Chapter 10 and Chapter 8 journey arithmetic, there are no horizon, residency, ownership or option-placement rules, no composition argument or interaction analysis per journey, and no allocated journey budget. The trace stops at controls and runtime plans: assurance records, obligations and actions sit outside the canonical graph, and the realization chain has no deployable unit or data store. Patterns and reference architectures can be adopted only as playbook ids or text, and outside the guided topics adoption imports no prerequisites, companions, tests or roles. Exceptions never expire or name an authority, and conditional approvals carry conditions as untracked text. Sol stays inside the doctrine, but its proposals carry no validation plan. Each gap below can be closed with person-authored records, deterministic checks and Sol proposals that a person accepts, without letting an LLM or candidate knowledge decide anything. Items that are already deep-dive gaps (decision rights, enterprise conformance, the RAID register, the causal trail, views) are cited as dependencies, not repeated.

### X-01 · No shared, claim-scoped evidence model: no common evidence class or level, no required level per claim, no 'does not establish' boundary and no inconclusive, invalid or blocked result  
*Severity high · effort L*

- **Handbooks:** Every result is labelled by evidence class or level: assumption, calculation, simulation, documented behaviour or guarantee, design review, local executed, target integration, load or fault exercise, or production observation. A gate compares the available level with the level the claim requires, and offline evidence against a target claim reads NOT_DEMONSTRATED. Each result records its claim, method, configuration and environment identity, population, observations and limitations, plus an explicit boundary of what it does not establish and which target qualification remains open. Runs can be invalid, blocked or inconclusive, failed attempts are kept, and no evidence state silently becomes the next.

### X-02 · Validate has no 'unknown' or 'unproven' result: missing, stale or unobserved evidence never blocks readiness, coverage is not counted, and assurance layers are only partly separated  
*Severity high · effort L*

- **Handbooks:** Gates are conjunctions of explicit predicates with criticality, each satisfied, violated or unknown. A rule reads PASS only when complete, current scope satisfies it. A witnessed violation is FAIL even when other observers are missing. Missing, stale or insufficient evidence is UNKNOWN, and a low-volume canary or missing denominator is INCONCLUSIVE; neither is ever folded into a pass, a neutral score or a reduced denominator, and an unknown critical predicate blocks the gate. Observations are reconciled against an independently derived expected population, NOT_APPLICABLE needs a rule and a reason, and silence never counts as success. Structural validity, factual support, runtime conformance and outcome benefit are separate layers, never collapsed into one green status. A missing link is unknown unless completeness is justified.
