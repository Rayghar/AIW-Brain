# Chapter 5 · Application realization (components)

Part of the AIW handbook gap review (AIW-HANDBOOK-GAP-REVIEW-2026-10-30.md). Gaps verified against the AIW source (paths relative to AIW-V5-Local-Source) and challenged by a skeptic; handbook references are "Handbook › heading (section id)".


**How AIW compares with the handbooks.** Chapter 5 covers the logical half of the handbooks' method well. It allocates responsibilities with a role and scope, records owners, boundaries and owned data, requires a failure policy on every flow interaction, and flags unmapped, orphan and doubly-owned elements. It also assembles a ten-section component specification from Chapters 2-10, with each field marked recorded, inferred or open. The handbooks treat every adopted mechanism as a governed bundle. That bundle holds fit criteria tested against the case, quality effects with measures and costs, scoped parameters with a basis and the failure if set wrong, a bounded guarantee with dependencies and exclusions, owned roles and obligations, failure modes with detection, and conformance tests. AIW builds that bundle in miniature, deterministically and as proposals only, inside the guided design tasks for eight topics (publication, resilience, traffic, partitioning, structure, interaction, reads, persistence) and released concerns. There, applicability predicates return known, unknown or blocked; every option becomes a Chapter 3 alternative with benefit, cost and counterfactual; roles expand into components and interactions; and obligations carry verification into Chapter 11. None of this is reachable from the ordinary component path. A pattern attached through Playbook patternIds, Chapter 3 free text, a library entry or an archetype creates no fit record, obligations, parameters, role check, guarantee boundary or tests. The 328 catalogue records that could feed these are templated text in 318 cases. Two structural objects the handbooks rely on are also missing as records. The first is the application deployable, which packages components with an evidenced split reason. The second is the atomic write set, with its concurrency protocol and writer inventory, which binds invariants to components. So nothing catches a service separated without reason or a split that crosses an invariant. Cross-mechanism composition is checked for only one Chapter 8 rule family. Failure behaviour is scattered across interactions, contracts, controls and plans, with no per-component register, no authored walkthrough and no discipline for derived copies. The governance posture matches the handbooks: library content never scores, Sol only explains or drafts and its guarantee wording is filtered, and a person accepts every proposal. The recommendations therefore lift task answers into durable Chapter 5 records (applied pattern, deployable, atomic set, composition) on the existing paths: component and property rows, Chapter 8 invariants and contracts, and the Chapter 11 obligation and verification chain. Validate checks these records and the SDD prints them, and no parallel authority is added.

### C5-01 · Adopting a pattern, style or library entry records no fit assessment outside the fixed design tasks: conditions held, contraindications, prerequisites, evidence needed, variant and simpler alternative  
*Severity high · effort L*

- **Handbooks:** A pattern, mechanism or building block is adopted only after its use-when, avoid-or-reconsider-when and prerequisites are tested against the case, and the catalogue is consulted only after the failure mechanism has been described. Each conditional choice is resolved as condition → choice, with its contraindication and the evidence needed. A variant is chosen by its 'choose when' and trade-off, and a simpler alternative is compared. The record states which conditions hold, which are unverified, which would disqualify the pattern, the rejected candidates and an exit condition, and it is pinned to the catalogue version. Adopting a mechanism without its prerequisites is a design defect, and a choice never rests on popularity, a vendor feature name or an unqualified quality claim.

### C5-02 · Atomic write sets, concurrency control and writer inventories are not recorded per invariant or bound to components, and nothing checks a split that crosses an invariant  
*Severity high · effort L*

- **Handbooks:** Derive each consistency boundary from a business invariant with a counterexample. Name the atomic set: which records commit together on which authoritative writer (scoped idempotency record, instruction, reservation, outbox intent, audit reference) and which effects deliberately stay outside, such as a provider call dispatched from the outbox. Choose a concurrency protocol. Optimistic control needs a version scope covering the invariant, an atomic compare-and-update, a retry bound and a conflict policy. Pessimistic control needs a lock scope, one deterministic lock order, a wait budget and no network call inside the transaction. A long hold needs a durable reservation with guarded confirm, release and expire. Inventory every writer, including admin, repair and scheduled paths, enforced by credentials. State the idempotency-key scope and the read consistency per operation, and resolve an ambiguous commit by querying the retained intent. Responsibilities that share an invariant stay in one transaction or carry an explicit coordination contract.
