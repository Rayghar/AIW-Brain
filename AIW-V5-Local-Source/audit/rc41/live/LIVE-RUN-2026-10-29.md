## Erratum (2026-10-29, after the improvements review in `IMPROVEMENTS-2026-10-29.md`)

The text above is kept as published. Four of its statements are corrected here:
- **Who withheld the four field drafts.** It was not "the deterministic grounding checks" in all four cases. Three were withheld by
  the deterministic guard: Chapters 1 and 4 for an unsourced "24", and Chapter 3 for guarantee wording. Chapter 10 was withheld by
  the model-based source check ("invented measurement or verification details"). Figures of 12 or less are exempt from the
  deterministic number check (`intelligence-provider.js` `guardFieldDraft`).
- **Journey coverage.** The draft held 24 records, but the packet sent to Sol carried only the first 12 of them: 53 of their 109
  fields, and none of Chapter 1's requirements, constraints or scope boundaries (`intelligence-context.js` `journeyPacket`).
- **"Grounded" field drafts.** Three of the seven accepted drafts (Chapters 7, 8 and 9) answer nothing. They restate the record's
  "Open question" placeholder at greater length, and accepting one would close the open question and drop it from the SDD. They
  should have been withheld or returned as "nothing to draft", so "what this shows" overstated the drafts' usefulness.
- **The probe's own counts.** The evidence file reads `withheld: 0` for field and journey runs whatever the outcome, and
  `groundingAccepted` means different things in different modes.

All four are tracked as fixes in the review.
