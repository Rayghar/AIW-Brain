# Round 19 · decision discovery, comparison, canvas and case review

## What changed

- **Design resources** is a project-scoped, bookmarkable reading of the existing Component library, Technology products and Knowledge repository. Technology search covers product, capability, quality driver, rationale and decision state; the selected product, filters and section are encoded in the URL. The product reading leads with its actual project option, decision state, reason, driver effects, evidence and version warning before detailed specifications. Chapter 5, Chapter 7 and Sources remain the record and review authorities. Browser Back and Escape restore the prior context and focus. Small screens use one full-width list or detail pane at a time.
- **Chapter 7** leads the comparison with capability, inherited quality scenarios, current decision state and rationale. An aligned comparison keeps each option's effect, reason and evidence against the same driver; specifications and operating obligations remain available. A source-version mismatch is visible at the option and record-selection point. The read-only target-change preview reuses the existing architecture branch, showing recorded and hypothetical inputs, option judgements to revisit, evidence gaps, linked records and a review plan. It does not change a target or claim target attainment.
- **Decision state** is one domain reading across the catalogue, dossier, Stack, Platform, Chapter 7 export and SDD. A changed connected input or product source marks a prior selection as needing review and removes a current approval claim. Bundled documentation for a different product version is labelled as an applicability warning, including in the SDD.
- **Expanded Layers** foregrounds a useful connection family by default, then a selected object's direct links. All connections restores every exact line. The grouped overview remains without connector lines. The native connection menu keeps keyboard focus after a checkbox redraw and has phone-sized targets and forced-colors visibility. Existing relation targets and exports remain available.
- **Validate** adds Focused case as a third reading beside SDD readiness and Model integrity. It uses the existing Chapter 11 case evaluation and shows selected-case metrics, linked worklist, current and historical architect observations, independent/source-use judgement and a separate whole-project gate. Newly recorded observations retain bounded fingerprints of connected dependencies so a later reviewer can identify changed records; older observations explain that their prior dependency list was not retained. No original passages or measurement samples are copied into the snapshot.
- **Shared phone shell** stacks the chapter title and readiness disclosure, keeps saving/offline/conflict status compact without losing its full accessible announcement, and preserves the conflict recovery control. Focus outlines use a theme-specific token with more than 3:1 contrast. The product detail's disclosure hit area is 44px.

## Boundary and next evidence

The technology catalogue is a descriptive projection, not a declaration that a product is suitable or deployed. The hosted workbench cannot directly browse the laptop corpus without its repository service or a permitted selected-original transfer. A product option needs project-specific version, tier, topology, operating assumptions and evidence; numeric quality attainment needs a real observation. An independent architect must judge the focused payment case and permitted source use. The whole-project SDD baseline and approval remain a separate gate. Hosted provider execution, scheduled continuation and the previously failing bank-payment project still need authenticated service receipts; build and health checks alone do not establish them.
