## Summary

1. The product is still four page applications: Chapter 1, Chapter 2, Chapter 3 and Chapters 4–11 each load separately (186–211 requests, 1.4–1.7 s each on loopback), the current tab is lost on every crossing (Validate → Work, Validate → Model) and the two shells differ in 23 visible ways (table 4).
2. The object companion is beside the workspace in Chapters 4–5 and below it in Chapters 6–11, where it sits at the end of Validate pages that are 7,500–13,700 px tall (17–44 screens); in Chapter 4 its layout icon overlaps the "← Back" button and it nests a 4,510 px list into a 290 px box.
3. Recording a decision in Chapter 3 took 19 clicks with two dead ends: "Record decision" is enabled while its four preconditions are unmet (400 from the server), and saving a rationale opens a second, stacked "Review this design change" dialog with a mandatory checkbox; afterwards an "ADR‑001 changed" banner appears on every chapter.
4. Validate leads every chapter with the whole‑journey SDD block; the chapter's own checks start below the fold, the default filter can show an empty state while findings exist, "Review" on a finding only selects the object and drops focus, and the same Review desk is reachable from three places.
5. State honesty and feedback: a blank project already shows "sample objects may be illustrative", "CAPACITY 3,226 req/s" and "12 blocking findings"; adding one responsibility drops Chapter 4 readiness from 50 % to 0 %; the new record lands below the fold (or on page 2) with a 12–15 s toast; "Next: Confirm the project brief" does nothing in Chapter 1.
6. Sol's panel overlays the workspace in Chapters 1–3 but pushes it aside in 4–11, holds 109 focusable controls and 11 font sizes in 370 px, and its "Open the chapter work →" opens the Model tab.
7. Model toolbars fold to unlabeled icons at 768 px and at 200 % zoom; phone diagrams render text at an effective 7.4 px; the review desk keeps 189 text nodes at 10.5 px and 95 of 228 controls under 44 px on a phone.
8. Vocabulary is still coined and mixed (realisation/realization, three naming sets for the chapters, "Object lens", "backlinks", "ghost", "Stewards", "Walk the rounds"), with definitions only in the Chapters 4–11 About dialog.
9. Typography and colour are unsystematic: 12–14 distinct font sizes, 27–51 text colours and 11–18 button heights on one page.
10. What works: keyboard focus is visible on every stop, the skip link works, dialogs guard unsaved changes and return focus, offline saves keep the input and say so, exports download, and the Chapter 1 blank Model tab has a clear empty state.
