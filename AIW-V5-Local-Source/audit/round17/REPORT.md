## Remaining product acceptance

- An architect must confirm the real repository corpus, source rights, requirements, alternatives, and chosen architecture for each client project. The sample project illustrates the interaction, not a client approved design.
- A reviewed project needs explicit implementation, test, and operational evidence before AIW can claim conformance. The new dossier makes that boundary visible; it does not infer conformance from design data.
- Hosted recurring drafting, live provider operation, and a realistic novice/practitioner usability study remain separate acceptance work.
