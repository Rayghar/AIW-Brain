## Root cause of the previous “dumb Sol” experience

The most serious defect was semantic, not cosmetic: the early lifecycle stage could be mapped to an older architecture-stage enum before reasoning. Sol could therefore receive the wrong architecture task even when the screen title looked correct. This was compounded by broad context payloads and multiple visible assistant identities.

## Current Brain request receipt

Each visible Ask response now exposes a compact receipt containing:

- task;
- exact lifecycle stage;
- project revision;
- knowledge release;
- Kernel version;
- Cambridge ruleset;
- LLM authority policy;
- confirmation that no direct model mutation occurred.

## Expert-quality boundary

A coherent Brain is necessary but insufficient. The remaining proof must compare Sol with independent senior architects on realistic projects, measuring correctness, completeness, false positives, omissions, time, trust and architecture outcomes. The internal Agency Banking benchmark is valuable evidence but is not a substitute for an independent human panel or a live enterprise initiative.
