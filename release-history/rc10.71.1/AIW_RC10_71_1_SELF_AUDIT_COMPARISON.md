## Non-patch test

The change passes the non-patch test because:

- old browser engines were deleted, not hidden;
- architecture routes were migrated, not wrapped with a second fallback;
- canonical repository loading and revision checks replace arbitrary posted project state;
- one receipt contract is reused across surfaces;
- historical evidence was separated from active evidence;
- intentionally shared packages have exact source parity.

The release does retain bounded helper implementations such as design-brief analysis and synthesis. They are now dependency-injected adapters behind the Brain Orchestrator. Deleting and rewriting them would add risk without reducing authority duplication.
