# AIW rc.10.71.1 Known Limitations

Generated: 2026-07-13T12:00:45Z

## Authority and knowledge

- The Architecture Knowledge Manifest is fingerprinted but not signed by an enterprise KMS.
- `CAMBRIDGE-SA-1.0` remains candidate-calibrated; the complete Cambridge corpus has not completed independent claim review, four-eyes promotion and production calibration.
- Pattern DNA, Cambridge, repository-derived claims and enterprise policies are coherently pinned in the receipt, but are not yet one physically compiled and independently signed knowledge artifact.
- Mind Factory still contains specialist extraction/promotion services. This is intentional supply-chain separation, but their own production acceptance remains open.

## Kernel and orchestration

- The Brain Orchestrator is the single runtime entry point, but deterministic algorithms remain implemented in multiple engine modules. They are governed through one entry path, not rewritten into one monolithic class.
- Some older UI component names such as `CoArchitectPanel` remain as interaction-surface names even though they no longer represent separate intelligence authority.
- Live provider use was not rerun in the final packaging environment. Deterministic fallback and governed provider contracts were regression-tested.

## Context Graph and impact

- Precise semantic impact requires explicit requirement, journey and evidence lineage. Legacy model objects without those references cannot be invalidated precisely.
- The graph does not yet represent every atomic source claim, enterprise policy clause, runtime observation, fitness-test result or SDD paragraph.
- Requirement change detection currently focuses on title, statement, priority, status, acceptance criteria and quality hints.
- Complex cross-branch merge, supersession and temporal graph history need further hardening.

## Requirements reconciliation

- Duplicate/contradiction detection is heuristic and deliberately produces candidate conflicts for human adjudication.
- It does not perform legal interpretation, formal logical entailment or full atomic-claim semantic diff.
- Source version replacement and conflict resolution are auditable, but a dedicated side-by-side claim comparison workspace remains open.
- Binary XLSX ingestion and OCR for image-only PDFs remain outside this increment.

## Acceptance

- The complete 396-requirement evidence matrix is not delivered; only the rc.10.71.1 delta is traced.
- Edge, Firefox and WebKit acceptance, complete keyboard/screen-reader/WCAG 2.2 AA verification and 200% zoom remain open.
- Large-model acceptance at 1,500 nodes and 5,000 relationships remains open.
- Product-shell and artifact-generation bundles remain large and emit a build warning.
- Managed OIDC, PostgreSQL tenant RLS, durable queues, object storage, KMS, OTLP telemetry, backup/restore and worker recovery require live enterprise acceptance.
- The Agency Banking golden benchmark, blinded expert review, measured human time baseline and controlled enterprise pilot remain incomplete.
