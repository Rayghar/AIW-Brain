## Executive result

rc.10.72.3 preserves the recovered one-Sol user experience and makes Sol's intelligence inspectable and testable. Each response now carries exact lifecycle context, deterministic reasoning, governed-knowledge contribution, optional LLM contribution, lineage and an eight-dimensional quality receipt. Low-quality LLM candidates are rejected rather than displayed as authoritative advice.

The controlled deterministic calibration passed **9 of 9 lifecycle scenarios** with an average score of **91.6/100**, **100% stage alignment**, and **zero unsupported numeric or legal claims**. A live provider was not configured, so no claim is made that governed LLM enrichment improves the deterministic result. An external human panel has not yet scored the blinded pack.

## Principal delivery

1. Exact stage-specific Sol context instead of generic project dumps.
2. Inspectable context, reasoning, quality and lineage receipts.
3. Eight response-quality dimensions with critical gates.
4. Automatic rejection of weak or unsupported governed-LLM candidates.
5. Stage-specific deterministic diagnoses across Requirements to SDD.
6. Domain-sensitive Pattern DNA calibration for regulated finance, event-driven designs and Data Mesh.
7. Governed Sol outcome capture for corrections and rejection evidence.
8. Nine-stage calibration lab, evidence exports and blinded expert-review pack.
9. Live-provider acceptance harness that reports a truthful blocked state.
