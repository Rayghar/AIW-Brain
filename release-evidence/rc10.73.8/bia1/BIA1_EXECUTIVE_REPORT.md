# BIA-1 executive report

## Decision

The AIW Brain is intelligent enough today for bounded, governed architecture co-creation in development. It is not production-accepted, and it does not yet demonstrate a large pure-reasoning advantage over a strong generic model.

After the one permitted remediation, AIW generated complete candidate architecture packages for all five frozen scenarios. The corrected frozen evaluator scored AIW Full Brain at 96.20, generic GPT-5.6 Sol at 95.18 and deterministic-only at 79.75. All five Full Brain scenarios passed the absolute gates; Agency Banking was strongest at 99.06 and Event-driven Fulfilment was weakest at 93.76.

The remediation replaced an oversized 430k–582k-character runtime transfer with a deterministic 63k–86k bounded projection that preserved every scenario requirement and the relevant Brain obligations. Full Brain generation moved from five local pre-provider failures in Pass 1 to five completed governed responses in Pass 2.

## What AIW can do reliably

- Turn bounded architecture requirements into coherent context, component, interface, data, security, resilience, deployment and transition views.
- Produce candidate decisions, trade-offs, risks, roadmaps, SDD sections and fitness tests.
- Preserve exact critical requirement traceability and interface ownership.
- Combine deterministic Brain contributions with governed model reasoning while keeping approved records and the canonical Design Graph unchanged.
- Replay the bounded context deterministically and retain candidate-only authority.

## What remains unreliable

- AIW's reasoning score is only 1.02 points above the generic model; its distinctive value is governance and lifecycle integration rather than a large architecture-quality lead.
- In the two browser journeys, all tested design-stage panels lacked an actionable generated candidate after recomputation. The benchmark API path and the current UI stage experience are therefore not yet aligned.
- Deferred specialist capabilities—such as Pattern DNA, Architecture Genome, contradiction synthesis and whole-reference-architecture assembly—remain outside this acceptance claim.

## Product-specific value

AIW contributes governed requirement and decision lineage, deterministic rule and review inputs, candidate Design Graph context, replayable context fingerprints, authority isolation, integrated governance and SDD workflow. Deterministic-only scoring of 79.75 also shows that the non-LLM Brain contributes substantial structure, although it does not meet the full intelligence gates by itself.

## Verification and limits

Ten provider calls completed with zero retries: five generic Pass 1 calls and five Full Brain Pass 2 calls. Observed usage was 113,097 input tokens, 128,085 output tokens and 241,182 total tokens. Exact billed cost is not claimed because no verified price receipt was captured. No human reviewer participated; the review authority was delegated GPT-5.6 Sol development adjudication only.

The recommended progression is Prompt 7, carrying three priorities: connect governed reasoning to actionable UI design-stage candidates, make the AIW-specific advantage more visible and measurable, and retain deferred specialist semantic capabilities as explicit backlog rather than overstating coverage.
